## Where to look every week

- [roboticsjobs.co.uk](https://roboticsjobs.co.uk) is UK-only and robotics-only.
- Glassdoor UK and LinkedIn with the search "robotics software engineer C++ ROS2".
- The careers pages of the companies above; most use Greenhouse, Lever or Workable, which
  let you set alerts.
- Cambridge is the densest cluster (Ocado is not there, but CMR, Dogtooth, Arm, Amazon, Five AI,
  Cambridge Consultants and TTP are). London second, then Oxford and Bristol.

## How to prioritise

1. Apply first where your existing Python robotics project maps onto their product: mobile
   autonomy companies if it was navigation, manipulation companies if it was arms, perception
   companies if it was vision.
2. Ocado, Wayve, Oxa, Dexory, CMR and Humanoid run the most structured C++ coding rounds. Those
   are the ones this tutorial was built for. Finish chapters 01 to 04 before applying to them.
3. Consultancies and defence primes usually interview on fundamentals (chapter 02) and a system
   design chat rather than LeetCode-style problems.
